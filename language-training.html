Turkish / Türkçe

Turkish for Beginners

A first conversation. A connection to culture.

Introduce your child to Turkish through useful everyday language and an introduction to Turkish culture. The focus is on taking those first steps: recognising familiar words, understanding simple questions and finding the confidence to respond.

What your child can explore

Listening & speaking

Greetings, introductions, polite expressions and simple questions about everyday life.

Vocabulary & pronunciation

Explore words for family, school, colours, numbers and daily routines while practising Turkish sounds.

Early reading & writing

Become familiar with the Turkish alphabet, recognise common words and build short written phrases.

Turkish culture

Discover how language connects to everyday customs, hospitality and respectful ways of addressing others.

Learn with moral education

Polite greetings, taking turns in conversation and showing interest in other people connect language practice with respect and consideration.

Developing responsible and respectful individuals.

A possible classroom activity

A short greeting dialogue can lead into introducing a friend, listening to their answer and practising a polite thank-you. A simple cultural example gives the conversation context.

Available in person in Dubai or as a live online course.

English

English for Beginners

Useful English. Thoughtful communication.

Help your child begin using English to introduce themselves, talk about familiar topics and understand simple instructions. Language learning is connected to moral values and principles, so learners practise how to express an idea and how to listen respectfully.

What your child can explore

Listening & speaking

Practise introductions, everyday questions, classroom expressions and short conversations.

Words for everyday life

Build vocabulary around home, family, school, feelings and familiar activities.

Reading & writing foundations

Explore letter sounds, common words and short sentences through guided reading and writing practice.

Moral values & principles

Use age-appropriate examples to explore kindness, honesty, responsibility and respect while building English vocabulary.

Learn with moral education

Simple language situations offer opportunities to ask for help politely, listen without interrupting and consider how words affect others.

Developing responsible and respectful individuals.

A possible classroom activity

Learners might read a short story about helping someone, discuss a character’s choice using simple English and practise a helpful response with a partner.

Available in person in Dubai or as a live online course.

Arabic / العربية

Arabic for Beginners

From the first letter to a confident voice.

Introduce your child to Arabic with a focus on reading, writing and recitation. Beginner learning connects the shape of a letter with its sound, giving children a foundation for recognising words and reading aloud with care.

What your child can explore

Letters & sounds

Become familiar with Arabic letters, their sounds and how letter shapes change within words.

Reading foundations

Practise recognising letters and building towards simple words and short passages appropriate to the learner’s level.

Writing practice

Explore right-to-left writing, letter formation and joining letters through guided practice.

Recitation & pronunciation

Develop careful listening and clear pronunciation through repetition and supported reading aloud. Ask us which recitation materials are used in the course.

Learn with moral education

Patient practice, attentive listening and respect for others are part of the learning approach. Language lessons support responsibility and consideration alongside reading and writing.

Developing responsible and respectful individuals.

A possible classroom activity

A lesson might introduce a letter and its sound, practise writing it, find it in a simple word and finish with guided reading aloud.

Available in person in Dubai or as a live online course.

For parents & guardians

A few questions before you begin.

Does my child need previous knowledge?

These courses are for beginners. If your child already knows some words or has studied the language before, let us know when you enquire.

Are all ages taught together?

Our overall age range is 7–17. Contact us with your child’s age so we can explain the available groups and recommend an appropriate option.

What does moral education mean in a language lesson?

It connects language practice with everyday values such as respect, honesty, kindness and responsibility. For example, children can practise asking politely, listening to others and discussing thoughtful choices.

What does Arabic recitation include?

The beginner focus includes pronunciation and supported reading aloud. Please ask us about the texts and recitation materials used, especially if you are looking for a particular type of recitation.

How long are the courses and what do they cost?

Contact us for current course duration, lesson frequency, fees and availability. These details are confirmed before enrolment.

How do I enquire about a trial class?

Use “Enquire about” for your chosen language. Enter your details, review the terms and continue to WhatsApp, where you can review and send your message. A request does not confirm a booking.

How quickly will my child progress?

Progress depends on age, starting point, attendance and practice. The aim is to build a foundation step by step; we do not promise fluency within a fixed number of lessons.
